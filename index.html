Don't Pick On Me

Poke the mushrooms. They poke back.

Shromp is a very large, very smug mushroom with no interest whatsoever in being picked. He lives in a little patch with five Shroomies, who mostly stand around looking unimpressed until somebody comes along.

In the scene above, hover over anyone and they react. Click Shromp and he takes a nudge, loses his cap or bursts into tears. Click the Shroomie here and it shrinks away, then slowly grows back. It all runs live from one small Rive file.
