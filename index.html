Don't Pick On Me

Poke the mushrooms. They poke back.

Shromp is a very large, very smug mushroom with no interest whatsoever in being picked. He lives in a little patch with five Shroomies, who mostly stand around looking unimpressed until somebody comes along.

Hover over anyone and they react. Click Shromp and he takes a nudge, loses his cap, or bursts into tears. Click a Shroomie and it shrinks away, then slowly grows back.

Every Shroomie shares one rig and gets its own cap and body colour at runtime, so the whole cast lives in a single lightweight file and runs live in the browser.
